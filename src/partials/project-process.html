Working with us

How Buying {{ project_name }} Through Us Works

We're an authorised Sattva channel partner, paid by the developer. You pay Sattva's own price, and you get someone on your side from the first question to the registration office.

Brief

Tell us your budget, the home or plot size you need, your timeline, and whether it's to live in, rent out or hold.

Live shortlist

We check {{ project_name }}'s current availability with Sattva's team and send matching units with the official price sheet and a full cost estimate.

Site visit

We book a slot with the site team, bring plans with open units marked, and walk the shortlist with you, in person or on video.

Paperwork

We go through the RERA record, approvals and draft agreement with you, and coordinate with lenders if you need a loan.

Booking to registration

You pay Sattva directly. We follow up on the agreement, payment milestones and registration date until the deed is in your name.

About Sattva Group

Sattva Group began in Bengaluru in 1986 as Salarpuria Sattva and has grown into one of South India's larger developers, with homes, office campuses, co-working, hospitality and data-centre projects in its portfolio. It carries "AA Stable" credit ratings from CRISIL and ICRA.

Its residential range runs from compact studios near the airport to luxury low-density projects and large plotted layouts. {{ project_name }} is one of {{ project_count }} Sattva communities we currently represent; you can compare them all on our projects page.
