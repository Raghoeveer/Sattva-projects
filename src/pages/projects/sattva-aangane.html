Sattva Aangane: Courtyard Homes With No Shared Walls

"Aangane" is Kannada for courtyard, and the whole project is planned around one: a two-acre central green, ringed by eight towers in which every home is open on three sides. These are large 3 and 4 BHK homes and duplexes on 10 acres along NH-75. Sattva is taking expressions of interest ahead of the RERA launch.

Three Ideas Behind Aangane

Open on three sides

No home shares a wall with another. That means cross-ventilation in every room, more daylight and less sound coming through from neighbours.

🌳

A real courtyard

Two acres of central green, not a podium garden on a car-park roof. The towers face inwards onto it.

🪜

Duplex residences

Signature 4 BHK duplexes with an internal staircase and their own lounge: something close to a villa, inside a tower.

How an EOI Works, and What to Watch For

An Expression of Interest gets you priority at launch. It isn't a booking, and the project isn't RERA-registered yet.

Register interest

Pay a refundable EOI amount (confirm in writing that it's refundable) to join the priority list.

Launch & RERA

Pick your unit

In depth

Where Aangane Fits in the Luxury Market

Space over spectacle

Much of Bengaluru's luxury launches compete on height and clubhouse size. Aangane's pitch is quieter: homes with no shared walls and light from three sides, around a real two-acre courtyard. It's aimed at buyers who'd otherwise consider a villa but don't want to manage a standalone house.

Low density, by design

Ten acres with 80% kept open and only eight towers makes for a much lower density than most East Bengaluru launches. The sky decks on every tower mean residents don't all crowd one clubhouse.

Why Old Madras Road

The NH-75 stretch beyond KR Puram has grown from a highway into a residential corridor serving Whitefield, ITPL and the airport link roads. Large land parcels are still available here in a way they aren't in Whitefield itself, which is what makes a 10-acre, low-density project possible.

The developer's track record nearby

Sattva already has a large presence on this corridor with its Songbird township at Budigere Cross. Buyers can visit completed and under-construction Sattva work a short drive away to judge quality before committing to Aangane.

Signature homes

The 4 BHK Duplex: A Villa in the Sky

How the duplex works

Two floors linked by a private internal staircase. Typically the lower level holds the living, dining, kitchen and a guest bedroom, while the upper level holds the family bedrooms and a private lounge. It feels like a house rather than a flat, with the security and services of an apartment.

Who buys duplexes

Larger families, households with elderly parents who want a separate floor, and buyers trading down from a villa who still want space and separation between living and sleeping areas. Supply is limited, so EOI holders usually get first choice.

Master plan

How Aangane's Eight Towers Are Arranged

Around the central green

The eight towers ring a two-acre landscaped green, with the clubhouse and lap pool near the centre. Most homes look inward over the green or outward over the edges of the site.

Sky decks on every tower

Each tower has its own elevated amenity deck, so residents have a nearby space for exercise or relaxing without walking to the main clubhouse.

Sports at the edges

Tennis, pickleball and other courts sit towards the periphery, keeping noise away from the homes facing the central green.

Themed gardens throughout

Reflexology walks, an aroma garden, a musical garden and a whisper grove are spread through the landscaping, not clustered in one corner.

Our honest take

Should You Buy at Sattva Aangane?

Aangane is Sattva's answer to buyers who've outgrown apartments but don't want the upkeep of a villa. Three-side-open homes with no shared walls, a real two-acre courtyard and a 40,000 sq ft clubhouse are a strong package on Old Madras Road.

It's still pre-launch, though: no RERA number, no possession date and prices that can change at launch. At ₹2.81 crore and up, the stakes are high.
