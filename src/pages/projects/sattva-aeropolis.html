Phase 1 or Phase 2: Which Suits You?

The two phases share the clubhouse and grounds, but they're aimed at quite different people.

Phase 1: for living in

1, 2 and 3 BHK homes, with RERA completion due in July 2026. It's the better pick if you want to move in sooner and need two or three bedrooms. That date has now passed, so ask us for your tower's actual handover and occupancy certificate status.

554 compact homes: studios of 448–500 sq ft and 1 BHKs of 550–658 sq ft, with possession due in October 2028. It has the lowest ticket size in the township.

Its pitch is rental demand from the aerospace park, the airport and the tech campuses coming up along NH-44. Small units also make the construction-linked payment schedule easier to manage.

In depth

Reading Aeropolis Properly

Aeropolis is easy to sum up as "cheap homes near the airport", but the details matter more than the headline. Here's what we walk buyers through.

One township, two products

Phase 1 is a conventional family project of 1, 2 and 3 BHKs. Phase 2 is a separate, denser block of 554 studios and 1 BHKs on a smaller slice of land, roughly 1.7 to 2.4 acres depending on the source. They share the clubhouse and grounds, but they'll attract quite different residents.

If you're buying to live, Phase 1's mix means more owner-occupier families around you. If you're buying to rent, Phase 2's compact units are designed for that, and you'll have a lot of fellow landlords as neighbours.

MIVAN construction, explained

Phase 1 uses aluminium formwork (often called MIVAN), where walls and slabs are cast in concrete together. The result is a monolithic structure that's fast to build, consistent in quality and less prone to the cracks that brick-and-plaster walls can develop.

The catch: almost every wall is structural. You can't remove or move walls later, and drilling for fittings needs care. Plan furniture and storage around the layout as it is.

The employment story

Aeropolis sits beside the KIADB Aerospace Park and close to the airport. Along NH-44 nearby, large tech and electronics campuses have been announced or are under construction. Those employers are the main reason tenants look here.

It's a different tenant base from Whitefield or ORR: more aviation, manufacturing and logistics, and more shift workers who value being close to work.

The density question

Around 1,001 homes on about 11 acres works out to roughly 91 per acre, high for G+11 towers. The practical effect is busier lifts, pools and parking at peak times, and a clubhouse that needs good management to keep up.

It also spreads maintenance costs across many owners, which helps keep monthly charges lower than in low-density luxury projects.

The full bill

What Gets Added to the Aeropolis Base Price

Typical extras

  • GST: 5% on under-construction homes; 1% if the home qualifies as affordable (carpet up to 60 sq m and value up to ₹45 lakh). Phase 1 units that already have an OC carry no GST
  • Stamp duty: roughly 5.6% including surcharge and cess on homes above ₹45 lakh, less in the lower slabs
  • Registration: 1%
  • Floor rise on upper floors
  • Car parking, clubhouse, advance maintenance and corpus fund

Example: Phase 2 studio vs Phase 1 2 BHK

A ₹45.77 lakh studio could qualify for 1% GST and falls into a lower stamp duty band, so taxes and fees might add only around ₹2.5–3 lakh, plus developer charges.

An ₹81 lakh Phase 1 2 BHK attracts 5% GST if still under construction (about ₹4 lakh), around ₹4.5 lakh stamp duty and ₹81,000 registration, before parking and maintenance. Ask us to run the exact numbers for your unit.

For investors

Rental Thinking for Aeropolis

Who you'll rent to

Airline and airport employees, aerospace park staff, and workers at the manufacturing and tech campuses along NH-44. Many are single or couples, which suits studios and 1 BHKs.

What drives yield

Small units tend to deliver the highest gross yields because rents don't fall as fast as prices when homes get smaller. Furnishing a studio well adds noticeably to the rent you can ask.

What can go wrong

With 554 compact homes in Phase 2 alone, many owners will be competing for tenants when it completes in 2028. Rents will depend heavily on how fast the nearby employers hire.

We don't promise yields. Ask us for current rents of comparable homes in Devanahalli when you're ready to decide.

Living costs

Maintenance and running costs

Monthly maintenance is charged per sq ft and set by the developer, then by the residents' association after handover. In a dense, amenity-heavy project, it's usually moderate per home because costs are shared widely.

Ask for the current rate, what it covers (security, housekeeping, clubhouse, power backup for common areas, water treatment), and whether there's a separate charge for the clubhouse.

Also ask whether all amenities will be ready when your tower is handed over. In phased townships, some facilities can lag behind the first homes.

Layout notes

What Each Aeropolis Home Type Is Like Inside

Phase 2 studio (448–500 sq ft)

One open room with a kitchen counter and bathroom. Built for a single occupant and furnished letting; storage is the main constraint, so built-in wardrobes and a loft bed make a big difference to rent.

Phase 2 1 BHK (550–658 sq ft)

A compact separate bedroom with a small living area. The larger variants are noticeably more comfortable; it's worth paying for the extra space if you plan to live in it.

Phase 1 1 BHK (666–672 sq ft)

Larger and more conventional than Phase 2's 1 BHK, and closer to completion. A good choice for a couple's first home.

Phase 1 2 BHK (906–943 sq ft)

An efficient family flat. MIVAN walls mean the layout is fixed, so check that the second bedroom suits your needs as drawn.

Phase 1 3 BHK (1,012 sq ft)

A compact three-bedroom with two bathrooms, among the most affordable 3 BHKs from a branded developer near the airport. Rooms are modest, so measure for furniture.

Which is best value?

Per sq ft, the Phase 1 2 BHK and 3 BHK are the cheapest homes in the township. Per rupee of rent, the Phase 2 studio and 1 BHK typically do best.
