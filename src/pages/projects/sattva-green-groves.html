The green network

What the 17 Parks Actually Are

Each park has its own purpose, so different parts of the layout feel different. Buying next to the right one is worth thinking about.

🌲 Urban forest

Dense native planting: the closest thing to a wooded walk inside the gates.

🐦 Birds park

Planted to attract birds. Plots nearby get the quietest mornings.

🏃 Fitness park

Outdoor exercise stations along a loop. Good for the jogging crowd.

🐕 Pets & leisure park

Space set aside for dogs and casual hangouts, so pet owners aren't using the kids' areas.

👴 Elders park

Seating and gentle paths for older residents.

🏘️ Neighbourhood parks

Smaller greens spread through the plot clusters, so every street has one within walking distance.

🌿 Community park

The central open space for events, next to the clubhouse.

🌳 Native tree zones

Pockets of native species that shade the main roads.

In depth

Buying Land at Nelamangala

A gateway town

Nelamangala sits where Tumkur Road (NH-48) and the Mangaluru highway divide, just outside Bengaluru's limits. It's become one of the region's biggest warehousing and logistics hubs, with steady employment and a growing residential population.

Close to finishing

Green Groves' expected completion is September 2026, much sooner than Sattva's newer plotted launches. That means you can see the roads, parks and clubhouse largely built before you buy, and start building your own home sooner.

Why the parks matter for value

In a layout of 735 plots, the parks are what separate a pleasant neighbourhood from a grid of houses. With 17 of them spread across 45 acres, most plots are within a short walk of green space, and park-facing plots should hold a premium over time.

Plot or flat out here?

On the city's edge, plots often make more sense than apartments. You avoid years of maintenance on an empty flat, land values in growth corridors have historically held up well, and you can build when you're ready. The trade-off is no rental income until you build.

Choosing a plot

How to Pick the Right Plot in a 735-Plot Layout

Which park?

Next to the Birds Park or Urban Forest means quiet mornings. Next to the Fitness Park or community park means more activity. Choose based on how you'll live.

Road width

Plots on the 24 m avenues are easiest to reach but busier. Plots on 9–12 m streets are quieter and usually a bit cheaper.

Facing & corner

East- and north-facing plots carry a Vastu premium, corners an extra-frontage premium. If neither matters to you, that's where you save.
