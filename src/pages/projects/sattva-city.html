The big idea

What a "15-Minute City" Means Here

Sattva markets the project as a place where most daily needs sit inside the gates. In practice that rests on three things.

🌳

Two central parks

The towers ring two large parks rather than facing each other across a podium, which is where most of the 84% open space goes.

🏊

Four clubhouses

Spreading amenities over four buildings means shorter walks and less crowding than a single clubhouse serving 3,000-plus homes.

🛍️

Retail on site

A planned mall and multiplex inside the township. Check its timeline separately, since retail often opens after the first towers are handed over.

Example: a 1,710 sq ft 2 BHK

Base price ~₹1.79 Cr. Development charge (₹290 × 1,710) ≈ ₹4.96 L; maintenance and corpus ≈ ₹1.3 L; amenities ~₹4 L; generator, gas and legal ≈ ₹1.65 L; a 10th-floor rise ≈ ₹4.6 L. That's around ₹1.95 Cr before tax.

Then 5% GST on an under-construction home, Karnataka stamp duty of roughly 5–5.6% and 1% registration. The realistic all-in figure is in the region of ₹2.15–2.2 Cr. This is illustrative; we'll prepare the real sheet for your chosen unit.

Where the money goes

Payments go to the project's collection account in the name of Sattva City Private Limited, as stated on the booking documents. Never to a broker.

Pre-EMI cost

By the time the 11th floor roof is cast, about two-thirds of the price has gone out. If that's loan-funded, you're paying interest well before you can live in or rent the flat.

Lower floors pay sooner

Stages are tied to the tower's progress, not your floor, so everyone in a tower pays on the same timeline. Different towers can run months apart, though.

The investment angle

What Could Drive Sattva City's Value

The factors partners and analysts point to, with our view on how much weight each deserves.

Before the toll

Doddajala sits on the city side of the airport toll. Residents get the airport without paying the toll on every city trip, a small but permanent everyday advantage over projects further north.

Developer strength

Sattva carries "AA" credit ratings from CRISIL and ICRA. On a six-year build of 3,460 homes, the developer's balance sheet matters more than usual.

Open space premium

84% open land around two big central parks is rare at this density. Park- and lake-facing units should hold value better, which is why they cost more.

Jobs on the doorstep

ITC, Prestige Tech Cloud, Ecopolis and the IFCI Financial City are all within about 20 minutes, bringing tenants and future buyers to the area.

Metro, eventually

The airport metro line is planned along this corridor. It would help, but timelines for metro projects slip; don't make it the reason to buy.

The counterweight

With 3,460 homes in one township, resale competition will be stiff when the first owners sell. Unit choice (view, floor, tower) will matter a lot.

Choosing a unit

How to Choose Your Tower and Floor at Sattva City

In a 13-tower township, where your flat sits matters as much as its layout. These are the factors we go through with buyers.

Location within the township

  • Park-facing: the two central parks give the best long-term views, and they're priced accordingly
  • Lake-facing: where available, these add a water view that can't be built over
  • Near the mall: convenient once it opens, but busier and noisier
  • Near the main road: easiest access, but more traffic noise

Floor and phasing

  • Towers range from 16 to 21 floors; upper floors carry a floor-rise of about ₹30 per sq ft per floor from the 2nd
  • Ask which towers are in the first delivery phase; they'll finish earlier but face construction around them
  • Later towers may be quieter at handover but finish nearer the 2032 RERA date
  • Check which of the four clubhouses is nearest your tower

Township living

What Living in a 3,460-Home Township Is Really Like

More amenities, shared widely

Four clubhouses and 200+ amenities mean there's genuinely something for everyone, and costs are spread over thousands of homes, which keeps per-flat maintenance manageable for what you get.

A small town's politics

A residents' association for 3,460 homes is a big organisation. Decisions about maintenance, rules and spending take longer, and getting involved early helps shape how the township is run.

Years of construction

Towers will be delivered in phases. If yours is early, expect construction noise and dust nearby for a while. Ask Sattva how work areas will be separated from completed towers.

Children's social world

For families with children, a township this size means friends, sports teams and activities within the gates, which is one of the biggest draws for parents.

Security at scale

Multiple gates, many visitors and deliveries: ask about access control, visitor management apps and how security is organised across the township.

Resale competition

When owners start selling, there'll be many similar flats on the market at once. Units with a distinctive feature (park view, terrace, corner) will stand out.

Our honest take

Should You Buy at Sattva City?

Sattva City is the most ambitious project on this list: a self-contained, 50-acre township on the right side of the airport toll, with the kind of open space that's almost impossible to find closer in. For a family planning a long stay in North Bengaluru, it ticks a lot of boxes.

The price of that ambition is time and money. Possession is in 2032, add-on charges run into lakhs, and you'll be paying stage instalments or pre-EMI for years before you move in. The sheer size of the township also means a long construction phase and plenty of competition when owners start to sell.

Our view: buy here if you want a large, premium home in a township you'll live in for a decade or more, and you can comfortably carry the payments until 2032. Choose your tower and view carefully, since unit choice will drive resale more than anything else. If you need to move sooner or want lower costs, look at Vasanta Skye or Lumina.

The strongest reasons to buy

  • 84% open space and two big central parks
  • On the city side of the airport toll
  • Jobs at ITC, Prestige Tech Cloud and Ecopolis nearby
  • Unusual formats: garden units, terrace units, penthouses

The biggest reasons to pause

  • Possession not until February 2032
  • Add-on charges of roughly ₹11–15.7 lakh
  • Years of construction in a 3,460-home township
  • High price per sq ft for the corridor
