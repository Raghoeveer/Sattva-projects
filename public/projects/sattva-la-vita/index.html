The case for it

Row House vs Apartment vs Building Your Own

vs a big apartment

Your own entrance, garden and terrace, no lift queues, and no neighbours above or below you. You'll pay for more built area, but you get ground under your feet.

vs an independent house

No plot hunting, approvals or contractors to manage. Security, landscaping and the clubhouse are handled for you.

vs a large villa project

63 homes is small enough that neighbours actually know each other, and far closer to the city than most villa communities.

In depth

Living in a La Vita Row House

A basement changes everything

Most row houses park cars on the street or in a front setback. La Vita's homes have their own basement, so parking is out of sight and the ground floor can open fully onto the garden. The leftover basement space is valuable too: buyers often fit out a home theatre, gym, studio or wine room.

Facing options for Vastu-minded buyers

Plans exist for east-, north- and west-facing homes, each with its room arrangement adjusted to the direction. If Vastu matters to your family, ask which facings remain before falling for a particular plan.

Nearly finished

Construction was reported at about 78% in February 2026, with RERA possession in late 2026. Unlike most Sattva launches, you can walk through near-complete homes before buying.

Hennur's school belt

Hennur and Byrathi have become popular with families largely because of the international schools nearby. For parents, a row house here can mean short school runs and a garden, in one move.

Budgeting

Costs to Expect on a La Vita Row House

On top of the price

  • GST at 5% while under construction (none once the OC is issued)
  • Stamp duty roughly 5–5.6% and registration 1%
  • Clubhouse, maintenance deposit and corpus
  • Interiors: four floors and a basement is a lot of house to furnish

Timing can save GST

If the occupancy certificate is issued before you register, the sale is GST-free. On a row house at this price level, that can be a saving of many lakhs. Ask where the OC stands before you book.
