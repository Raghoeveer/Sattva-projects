In depth

Vasanta Skye, Explained

A township that spans every budget

Most airport-corridor projects pick a lane: compact homes for investors, or large homes for families. Vasanta Skye covers both, from a 454 sq ft studio to a 2,416 sq ft 4 BHK, inside one 16-acre gated community.

That broad mix tends to make for a more balanced community and a wider pool of buyers when you eventually sell, because you're not competing only with identical units.

Mid-rise, not high-rise

The towers are G+13 and G+14, noticeably lower than the 28–31 floor towers going up in other parts of Bengaluru. Fewer floors means shorter lift waits, less reliance on pumps and lifts for daily life, and more of a neighbourhood feel.

Eleven towers arranged as 16 wings around a landscaped central court keep roughly 80% of the land open.

Location on the airport side of NH-44

Kannamangala village is about 10 km from the terminal, close to NH-44 and SH-104. Devanahalli town is about five minutes away and the aerospace SEZ about eight, which puts many of the area's biggest employers within a short drive.

The planned Peripheral Ring Road would further improve links to the city's business districts if and when it's built.

Early buyer sentiment

On Google, the project currently averages around 4.1 out of 5. Reviewers mostly credit the location, the price plan and the range of amenities. Since nothing has been handed over yet, these ratings reflect the launch offer rather than finished homes, so weigh them accordingly.

Budgeting

A Worked Example and the Payment Path

Example: a ₹78 lakh entry home

On a ₹78 lakh base price, 5% GST adds about ₹3.9 lakh, Karnataka stamp duty roughly ₹4.4 lakh and registration ₹78,000. Developer charges (parking, clubhouse, maintenance, corpus, legal) typically add a few lakh more.

A realistic all-in figure is around ₹88–90 lakh. We'll prepare the exact sheet for your unit.

How payments usually run

  • Booking amount, then a top-up within a few weeks
  • A larger instalment when the agreement is signed
  • Stage payments as your tower's slabs are cast
  • Final instalments at finishing and possession, along with other charges
  • With possession in May 2031, plan for several years of stage payments or pre-EMI

Choosing a unit

Picking a Block, Wing and Floor at Vasanta Skye

Court-facing vs outward-facing

Wings that face the landscaped central court get a green, protected view and more community life below. Outward-facing units look over the surrounding land, which may be developed in future. Court-facing units usually carry a premium.

Which floor in a G+13 tower

With only 13–14 floors, even upper floors are a short lift ride. Middle floors (5th–10th) are often the sweet spot: above ground-level noise, below the highest floor-rise charges.

Mixing home types

Studios and 4 BHKs share the township but not necessarily the same wing. If you'd prefer neighbours who live in their homes rather than tenants, ask which wings are mostly larger units.
