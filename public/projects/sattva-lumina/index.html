How the site works

One gate, a clubhouse at the front, green down the middle

Lumina's master plan puts the clubhouse right beside the single entrance, so residents pass it every day. Visitors and deliveries don't need to go any further in than that.

Parking sits in two basement levels, which leaves a long, mostly car-free stretch of landscaping between the towers. The site also borders a lake and mature tree cover, so the lower floors of the end towers look onto greenery rather than other buildings.

In depth

Understanding Sattva Lumina

A middle-market township, on purpose

Plenty of North Bengaluru launches chase the luxury buyer with 3 and 4 BHKs above ₹2 crore. Lumina goes the other way. Its mix starts at a studio and tops out at a three-bathroom 3 BHK, and the whole project is pitched at people who want a well-run community without paying for a statement address.

That makes it a practical choice for young families, first-time buyers moving up from renting in Yelahanka or Hebbal, and investors who want a unit that tenants will actually look for.

Big enough to support a real clubhouse

At 1,549 homes on 13 acres, Lumina has the scale to fund a proper multi-level clubhouse and 40-plus amenities, and to spread their running costs across many owners. Smaller projects often struggle with that.

It's not so big that it feels anonymous, though. Eight towers around one green spine is a size most residents can get to know.

Why Rajanukunte

Rajanukunte sits just north of Yelahanka where the city starts giving way to lakes and farmland. Land here costs much less than in Hebbal or Thanisandra, which is how Lumina can offer a ₹38 lakh studio from a branded developer.

In return you're further out: daily trips to central Bengaluru are long. Lumina makes most sense if your work is north of Hebbal, or if you're buying to rent to people whose work is.

Budgeting

What a Lumina Home Really Costs

Base price is only part of it. Here's how the extras typically stack up, and one tax point that matters a lot for studios.

Typical add-ons to expect

  • GST: 5% on under-construction homes, or 1% if the home qualifies as "affordable" (carpet up to 60 sq m and value up to ₹45 lakh)
  • Stamp duty: Karnataka slabs, lower for homes under ₹45 lakh and roughly 5–5.6% above it
  • Registration: 1%
  • Car park, clubhouse, legal and generator charges
  • Advance maintenance and corpus fund
  • Floor rise on higher floors and PLC for preferred units

Why studios are worth a second look

A ₹38–45 lakh studio may fall within the affordable-housing GST rate of 1% instead of 5%, and into a lower stamp duty slab. On a ₹40 lakh home, that alone can save around ₹1.6 lakh in GST.

Whether a specific unit qualifies depends on its exact carpet area and final price, so ask us to check before you book.

First-time buyers

Buying Your First Home at Lumina

With studios from about ₹38 lakh, Lumina attracts a lot of first-time buyers. Here's what to know if this is your first purchase.

Your down payment

Banks usually lend up to 80–90% of a home's value, depending on price. On a ₹38 lakh studio, plan for a down payment of roughly ₹4–8 lakh plus stamp duty, registration and developer charges.

Tax benefits

Home-loan principal and interest can qualify for income-tax deductions under the applicable sections, subject to limits and the tax regime you choose. Check with your tax adviser.

Pre-EMI vs full EMI

While Lumina is under construction, you can pay only interest on the amount disbursed (pre-EMI), or start full EMIs straight away. Full EMIs cost more now but reduce total interest.

Getting a loan approved

Choose a bank that has already approved the project. It speeds up the loan because the bank has done its legal and technical checks.

Reading the agreement

Look for the possession date, delay compensation, specifications annexure, payment schedule and cancellation terms. We'll go through it with you.

Planning for interiors

Budget for wardrobes, a modular kitchen, lights, fans and appliances. Even a studio needs a few lakh to be fully livable or rentable.

Our honest take

Should You Buy at Sattva Lumina?

Lumina does something few North Bengaluru launches attempt: it makes a full Sattva township affordable, with studios from about ₹38 lakh and a complete clubhouse and green spine shared across 1,549 homes. For first-time buyers and investors, that's a rare combination.

Its weakness is geography. Rajanukunte is quieter and greener than Hebbal or Thanisandra precisely because it's further out, and daily trips to the CBD or Whitefield are long. The 30-floor towers also won't suit everyone.

Our view: Lumina is a strong pick if your life is centred north of Hebbal, if you work hybrid, or if you want a compact, rentable unit from a branded developer at a sensible ticket size. The January 2029 RERA date is also earlier than most comparable launches.
