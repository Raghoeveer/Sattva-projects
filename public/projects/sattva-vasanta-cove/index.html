In depth

Thinking Through a Vasanta Cove Purchase

Small project, specialised product

With only 200 homes, Vasanta Cove behaves more like a boutique building than a township. Its clubhouse, built around co-working and creator spaces, is designed for one kind of resident: the young professional who works partly from home.

Why the 1 BHKs are scarce

Only 37 of the 200 homes are one-bedroom. For a buyer who plans to live in the flat as a couple, that scarcity is useful: fewer identical units competing when you sell.

The tenant pool

Airport staff, aerospace park employees, and workers at Foxconn's upcoming facility and Prestige Tech Cloud nearby. Studios here are designed to be rented furnished to single professionals.

The waiting period

December 2030 possession means about five years of stage payments or pre-EMI before any rent comes in. Factor that holding cost into any return you calculate.

Budgeting

Studio Cost Example

A ₹59 lakh studio, all-in

GST at 5% adds about ₹2.95 lakh (a unit only qualifies for the 1% affordable rate if it's at or under ₹45 lakh and 60 sq m carpet). Stamp duty is roughly ₹3.3 lakh and registration ₹59,000. Add parking, clubhouse and maintenance charges.

A realistic total is about ₹68–70 lakh. We'll confirm with the actual cost sheet.

Rental return reality check

  • Furnishing a studio well: budget a few lakh
  • Monthly maintenance on a small unit is modest but ongoing
  • Expect gross yields in the low-to-mid single digits
  • Net yield is lower after vacancy, maintenance and tax
