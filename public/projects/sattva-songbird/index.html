The bigger picture

A small phase inside a big township

Phase 2 is only 4.33 acres, but it sits within the 50-acre Songbird township, which also includes independent row houses and further towers in other phases. You get a fairly intimate two-tower community with access to the wider township's parks and clubhouses.

The flip side is that other phases may still be under construction nearby after you move in. Ask us where Phase 2 sits on the overall plan relative to future work.

In depth

What Makes Songbird Phase 2 Distinct

Two-side open, every home

Each of the 381 homes has openings on two sides. In practice that means cross-breezes through the living spaces and more daylight than a single-aspect flat. It's worth checking on the plan which two sides your unit opens to.

Three clubhouses for one community

Rather than one huge building, Songbird's amenities are split across three clubhouses with 50+ facilities between them, from a golf putting lawn and tennis to a library and amphitheatre.

A wide price ladder

From a studio under 500 sq ft to a 3 BHK over 1,800 sq ft, Phase 2 covers first homes, rental investments and family upgrades in the same two towers.

Whitefield without Whitefield prices

Budigere Cross sits on Old Madras Road beyond KR Puram, a short drive from Whitefield and ITPL. Prices here are typically below comparable new launches inside Whitefield itself.

Budgeting

Worked Example: the Songbird 2 BHK

From ₹1.56 Cr to the real total

On a ₹1.56 crore base, 5% GST adds about ₹7.8 lakh, Karnataka stamp duty roughly ₹8.7 lakh and registration ₹1.56 lakh. Car parking, clubhouse, maintenance and corpus typically add several lakh more.

Expect an all-in figure in the region of ₹1.78–1.82 crore. We'll prepare the exact sheet for your unit.

Ask about

  • Floor-rise rate and from which floor it applies
  • PLC for corner and park-facing units
  • Number of car parks included
  • Which of the three clubhouses Phase 2 owners use
  • The RERA possession date for Phase 2
