In depth

What Makes Forest Ridge Worth a Closer Look

An established neighbourhood, not a frontier

Most new Sattva launches are on Bengaluru's edges, where you're buying into what an area might become. Forest Ridge is different. JP Nagar 9th Phase and Anjanapura already have schools, hospitals, malls, a metro line and decades of residential life around them.

You're paying for that certainty, and the price per sq ft reflects it. But you're not waiting years for the neighbourhood to fill in.

Tall and slim to keep the ground green

On just over four acres, Sattva has stacked 407 homes into three towers of up to 30 floors. Building upwards is what lets roughly three-quarters of the land stay as forest-themed landscaping rather than car parks and podiums.

The result is fewer homes per lift than in a sprawling mid-rise, and a green setting that ties in with the central park across the road.

A view that's protected

Anjanapura Central Park sits directly opposite. Homes facing it get an open outlook that can't be built over, which is rare in South Bengaluru. Those units cost more and sell first.

Units on the other sides look over the surrounding neighbourhood and are better value if the view matters less to you.

Who it suits

Families already living in JP Nagar, Banashankari or Jayanagar who want to upgrade without leaving the south; professionals working on Bannerghatta Road or in Electronic City via NICE Road; and buyers who want a branded new-build in an area where few such projects exist.

Budgeting

Planning the Full Cost of a Forest Ridge Home

Ask whether the quoted price includes…

  • GST (5% on under-construction homes)
  • Car parking and floor rise
  • Clubhouse membership
  • Advance maintenance and corpus fund
  • Power backup and gas connection
  • Legal and documentation charges

Stamp duty (roughly 5–5.6% above ₹45 lakh) and 1% registration are always extra.

Example: a ₹1.30 Cr 2 BHK

If ₹1.30 crore is the all-inclusive developer price, add about ₹7–7.3 lakh in stamp duty and ₹1.3 lakh registration, for roughly ₹1.39 crore at registration.

If the quote turns out to exclude GST and other charges, the figure rises by several lakh more. We'll confirm exactly what's included before you commit.
