Who runs this site

Narayanan Rajesh, RERA-registered agent

Sattva Projects is run by Narayanan Rajesh, a real estate agent registered with both Karnataka RERA and Tamil Nadu RERA, who has advised home and land buyers across Bengaluru for more than ten years.

Over that time, many of the families he has helped chose Sattva projects, from apartments near the airport to plots in Mandya. This site brings that experience into one place: current Sattva inventory, honest answers about each project, and a single point of contact from the first call to the registration office.

Being a channel partner means the developer pays our fee. It doesn't mean we stop asking awkward questions on your behalf.

Ground rules

What We Do, and What We Never Do

We always

  • Share the developer's price sheet in writing before you decide
  • Give you the project's RERA number and where to check it
  • Estimate stamp duty, registration and developer charges up front
  • Tell you when a unit or plot has a drawback we know about
  • Respond on the same day you contact us

We never

  • Collect booking money or payments in our own name
  • Charge buyers a fee on top of the developer's price
  • Promise guaranteed returns or appreciation
  • Push you to book on the day of a site visit
  • Share your details with anyone other than the developer handling your booking
