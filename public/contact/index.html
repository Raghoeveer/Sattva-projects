What Happens After You Get in Touch

How quickly will someone respond?
Usually within the hour during the day, and always on the same day. If you message late at night, expect a reply the next morning.
Will I be flooded with calls from multiple agents?
No. Your details stay with us and, when you're ready to book, with the developer's team handling your booking. We don't sell or pass on enquiries.
Can I visit the site without booking through you?
Of course. But if you'd like us to set up the visit, we'll arrange the slot with the site team and bring the list of open plots or units, which saves you time on the day.
